Privacy Policy

1. Information We Store

Cat Gatekeeper does not collect personal information.
This extension stores only the settings needed for it to work in your browser.

  • Target site settings
  • Usage time limit settings
  • Break time settings
  • Timer state

2. How Information Is Stored

Settings and timers are stored in your browser's local storage.
This information is not sent to external servers.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

This extension does not collect, sell, or transmit the following information.

  • Personal information such as your name or email address
  • Advertising IDs or device identifiers
  • Browsing history, page content, or SNS post content
  • Information that can identify you personally

4. External Services

This extension does not use external services for ads, analytics, or tracking.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

Cat Gatekeeper does not provide user information to third parties.

6. Contact

Please report bugs or suggestions through GitHub Issues.
For other inquiries, contact zokuzoku.app@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when necessary.
